\paragraph{Limitations.}
Rung 4 rests on two seeds and its band-limited arms are not comparable to full-band baselines; capacity, optimization or implicit regularization may explain the null. Semantic recovery is asymmetric; the linear-rung TIDMAD reversal is not replicated at a confirmatory file; stationary-PSD adequacy is per-instrument (adequate on the TIDMAD readout, $\chi^2/\mathrm{dof}=1.0031$, failing on GW strain); and the primary experiment's oracle covariance is narrowed, not removed, by one estimated-covariance condition --- no sweep over calibration sizes, covariance families or misspecification.
