\paragraph{Limitations.}
The ladder asks whether the likelihood geometry matters and how it interacts with the architecture, in closed form at the linear rungs and empirically at the factored-attention rung \PENDING{rung-4 verdict --- unrounded rescore}. Five caveats bound the evidence. The rung-4 claim is the paired contrast, not the rank: both band-limited arms sit far below the published baselines, and a null contrast would itself be a finding, since capacity can absorb a misspecified metric. Rung 4 is one training run per arm, so seed variance is uncharacterized. The linear-rung TIDMAD reversal is not replicated at a confirmatory file. Stationary-PSD adequacy is per-instrument --- adequate on TIDMAD ($\chi^2/\mathrm{dof}=1.0002$), failing on gravitational-wave strain. And the $C=8>k_c=2$ result is synthetic with an oracle covariance, so it bounds what a correctly specified $\Sigma$ buys, not an estimated one.

\paragraph{Takeaway.}
Label-free scientific reconstruction under structured noise should begin with the measurement likelihood rather than treating MSE as a neutral default: $(\Sigma^{-1},\mathcal{F})$ separates the metric from the architecture, the metric question recurs from the optimal filter to factored attention, and its answer is an empirical property of the instrument.
