\section{Evidence}

\paragraph{Metric reversal in the linear rungs.}
A 30-seed rank-two synthetic benchmark with white-noise control establishes the reversal at rung 2; on one public TIDMAD file with an injected reference \citep{fry2024tidmad} the weighted residual agrees with the recorded truth against raw MSE --- one file, not replicated at a confirmatory file (Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}); the consolidated evidence below supersedes these. TIDMAD's diagnostics show the signal is not a fixed low-dimensional subspace (the tone sweeps; one file is two-tone): the architecture binds; rung 4 lifts it.

\paragraph{NFPA under channel compression.}
Rung 3 is consolidated at $C=8>k_c=2$ channels, matched total rank $k=k_ck_t=6$: NFPA, an isotropically trained factored baseline of the same architecture, CW-PCA, and PCA, fitted on the same ten seeds. NFPA and the isotropic baseline share an architecture and differ only in the training metric, so their paired contrast isolates it. It reverses: NFPA is better under the likelihood residual (paired difference $-0.0192$, 95\% interval $[-0.0194,-0.0190]$) and worse under raw MSE ($+0.0034$, $[+0.0034,+0.0034]$), both excluding zero over ten seeds. Against the unrestricted CW-PCA the held-out likelihood residual differs by $-0.0026$ ($[-0.0026,-0.0025]$) --- a finite-sample difference, not evidence the Kronecker restriction is intrinsically superior. The twenty-seed white-noise run is an identity check: at $\Sigma=\sigma^2I$ the objectives coincide up to scale, and its exact zero ($0.000000$, $[0,0]$) verifies the pipeline introduces no spurious contrast.

\paragraph{Rung 4 (exploratory): the metric in a factored-attention network.}
As an exploratory case study (one training seed per objective; no across-run inference is claimed), one factored-attention architecture is trained on the TIDMAD readout with two objectives differing only in the metric --- \texttt{T-mse} on the unwhitened spectrogram, \texttt{T-chi2} on the $\Sigma^{-1}$-weighted residual --- at matched data, budget, and evaluation grid. \emph{In this pair of runs the reversal does not appear.} Over fifty held-out evaluations the $\Sigma^{-1}$-trained arm is better on \emph{both} criteria --- paired difference $-1.15\times10^{-4}$ on the normalized likelihood residual and $-57.2$ on raw MSE, the second opposite to prediction; the differences are stable along the trajectory (last-ten contrasts agree in sign), but only independent-seed replication can make this an architecture-level statement. The release's coarse denoising score does not separate the arms: paired difference $-1.0\times10^{-3}$, window and file-block bootstrap intervals $[-3.3,+1.4]$ and $[-4.6,+2.4]\times10^{-3}$, both spanning zero; both arms sit far below the published baselines (Appendix~\ref{app:rung4-baselines}; Figure~\ref{fig:evidence}). A degenerate metric would explain it, and we tested for one: $J$ spans a factor $441$ and the whitening weights have coefficient of variation $0.69$ --- the same order as the synthetic rung-3 geometry where the reversal \emph{is} observed ($1.10$; white control $0$) --- so the metric is comparably non-degenerate at both rungs, and the absence is a property of this instantiation, not of the ladder. Candidate accounts --- capacity absorbing the weighting, optimization-trajectory effects, implicit regularization --- are not distinguished by one seed per arm.

\paragraph{Transfer of the noise model.}
On a public gravitational-wave strain release \citep{gwoscgw150914,macleod2021gwpy}, a whitened score matching its reference form to $10^{-12}$ and calibrated on stationary synthetic noise (dispersion $1.15$) is seven to nine times over-dispersed on real H1 and L1 strain ($9.42$, $8.83$; leave-one-out $1.77$, $7.29$). A covariance model can be correctly implemented, validated synthetically, and still fail to transfer: implementation identity is not calibration; transfer is where a learned geometry is falsified.
