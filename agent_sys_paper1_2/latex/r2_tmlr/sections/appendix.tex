\section{Algorithms, manifests, and supplementary material}
\label{app:supplement}

This appendix documents the supplementary material accompanying the method
paper. All quantitative appendix content is generated from the same locked
analysis artifacts as the main text; the anonymous review build contains no
identifying URLs, filenames, or metadata.

\subsection{Algorithms and costs}

The appendix includes the complete algorithms for the value-of-information
and learned controller policies; the full action--cost definitions for every
action subtype (continue, verify, recover subtypes, escalate, abstain,
terminate); and the budget-accounting rules including budget-exhaustion
censoring.

\subsection{Randomization and propensity diagnostics}

The randomization records and blocking structure for the online controller
study (R2-C1) are provided, including the blocked order generation and the
episode-level random effects used when exact replay was impossible.
Propensity diagnostics for the verified-intervention dataset (R2-C2) report
the effective sample sizes under importance weighting, the support checks,
and the conservative unsupported-action sensitivity bound.

\subsection{Data and compute accounting}

The per-arm data and compute ledger reports source trajectories, effective
tokens, optimizer steps, evaluation budget, and verification cost for each
of the six post-training arms, with any unavoidable mismatch explicitly
noted. The checkpoint selection rule and its development-only provenance
are documented.

\subsection{Adjudication rubric and per-stratum results}

The postcondition rubric used by adjudicators is provided in full.
Per-task-template, per-fault-family, and per-training-seed breakdowns of all
primary results are reported, along with the robustness checks under
alternative cost weights and non-inferiority margins. Exclusions and
missingness by arm use the shared eight-way censoring classification.
The protocol deviations log records any change after the confirmatory
freeze.

\subsection{Anonymous reproducibility checklist}

The anonymous reproducibility checklist lists the exact commands to reproduce
the primary controller comparison, rebuild the verified-intervention dataset,
construct the training views with split isolation checks, and regenerate
every table and figure from the frozen analysis artifacts.
