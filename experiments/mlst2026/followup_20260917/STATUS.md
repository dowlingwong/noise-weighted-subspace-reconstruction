## R1 → R4 cancellation gate (A2)

R4's `n_files = 32/64` cells are admissible only if R1 yields a usable interval.
`r4_t22_feasibility/r1_gate.py` evaluates the precondition from R1's
`FROZEN_CANDIDATE.json` and `R1_ACCEPTANCE.json`:

* **cancel** if R1 froze `"none"`, or R1's acceptance receipt shows a coverage or
  type-I failure at any file count, or the withholding screen rules out every
  design;
* **unresolved** until R1 resolves; **pass** otherwise.

A cancel **omits** the large-file cells from R4's matrix, launch plan and cost
report and emits an `R4_INFEASIBLE` receipt — it is not a deferral. A test
(`tests/test_followup_20260917_r4.py`) refuses to mark R4 ready-to-transfer while
R1 records `"none"` or a failed interval, and a mutation test proves the refusal
fires.
