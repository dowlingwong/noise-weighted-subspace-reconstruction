  and passed in the run container (x86_64, numpy 2.4.6, scipy 1.17.1).  Left
  unchanged; recommended fix is to assert on `1 - cos` or use 1e-5 degrees, as
  the neighbouring tests already do.

  canonical renderings (regenerable pixel-identically from
