\section{Four representation classes}

Holding the metric fixed, we vary $\mathcal{F}$ across four classes, labelled rungs 1--4 for reference. The labels are \emph{not} a capacity ordering: rung 3 \emph{restricts} rung 2, rung 1 is its degenerate fixed-template case, and rung 4 is not comparable. Rungs 1--3 are stated in general two-axis form and evidenced synthetically; rung 4 uses the short-time Fourier transform (STFT) band--frame plane of TIDMAD~\mbox{\citep{fry2024tidmad}}, a public release of SQUID readout series from an axion dark-matter search, where injected signals must be recovered from instrument noise and $\Sigma^{-1}$ is diagonal in frequency by measurement (Section~4).

\paragraph{Rung 1: rank-one fixed template (optimal filtering).}
Fixing the signal to a known template $s_0$, the optimal filter (OF) gives the likelihood-optimal amplitude estimate $\hat A$ \citep{gatti1986processing,turin1960introduction}, with reconstruction $\hat s=\hat As_0$; the representation class is a single direction and the metric question has a closed-form answer.

\paragraph{Rung 2: unrestricted rank-$k$ linear.}
Replacing the fixed template with a learned rank-$k$ subspace gives covariance-weighted PCA (CW-PCA), which is also the tied noise-aware linear autoencoder: both minimize the likelihood residual over the full Grassmannian $\Gr(k,n_1n_2)$ of a two-axis measurement (Proposition~\ref{prop:bridge}), with reconstruction $\hat s=PP^\top\Sigma^{-1}x$ for a $\Sigma^{-1}$-orthonormal basis $P$.

\paragraph{Rung 3: Kronecker-separable two-axis projection (NFPA).}
For axis-aligned noise, $\Sigma=\Sigma_2\otimes\Sigma_1$, NFPA restricts rung 2's projection to separable factors of a two-axis measurement $Z_i\in\R^{n_1\times n_2}$ --- sensor channel $\times$ time in the controlled synthetic study, frequency band $\times$ time frame on the public benchmark. On the per-axis whitened observation $\tilde Z_i=\Sigma_1^{-1/2}Z_i\Sigma_2^{-1/2}$ it learns orthonormal bases $U_1\in\R^{n_1\times k_1}$ and $U_2\in\R^{n_2\times k_2}$, with code $C_i=U_1^\top\tilde Z_iU_2$ and reconstruction $\hat{\tilde Z}_i=U_1U_1^\top\tilde Z_iU_2U_2^\top$ mapped back through $\Sigma^{1/2}$; the joint representation is $\range(U_1)\otimes\range(U_2)$, and the admissible family is the embedded product $\Gr(k_1,n_1)\times\Gr(k_2,n_2)\hookrightarrow\Gr(k_1k_2,n_1n_2)$.

\begin{proposition}[Kronecker-restricted bridge]
\label{prop:restricted}
NFPA minimizes the CW-PCA/tied-autoencoder objective restricted to Kronecker-separable two-axis subspaces. Its optimum equals the unrestricted CW-PCA optimum if and only if at least one rank-$k_1k_2$ CW-PCA minimizer has such a separable subspace.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Proof sketch.}
In whitened coordinates the factored map is the orthogonal projector onto $\range(U_1)\otimes\range(U_2)$ (vec identity, Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}), so NFPA is the whitened rank-$k_1k_2$ objective restricted to that product; equality holds precisely when that set contains an unrestricted minimizer, and otherwise the gap is the cost of the architecture prior, not a failure of the metric --- a cost Appendix~\ref{app:protocol} measures by fitting rungs 2 and 3 at matched rank. This is a covariance-aware member of the multilinear-PCA family \citep{lu2008mpca}, not a new factorization. Even a full-rank factor leaves the restriction active: with $k_1=n_1$ the family is $\R^{n_1}\otimes\,\range(U_2)$, still separable. At $k_1=k_2=1$ rung 1 is recovered under Corollary~\ref{cor:rankone}'s assumptions (Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}).

\paragraph{Rung 4: factored attention.}
Rung 3's restriction on the band--frame plane reads as $\range(U_f)\otimes\range(U_m)$; rung 4's transformer performs intra-band temporal attention over frames, then cross-band attention. The attention model is an \emph{analogous axis-factored architecture} in the same coordinates, and $\Sigma^{-1}$ is diagonal in exactly the basis the first factor indexes: on this benchmark a stationary-PSD model fits the readout noise ($\chi^2/\mathrm{dof}=1.0031$; Section~4). A token-axis design principle follows: choose the token axis in which $\Sigma^{-1}$ is diagonal and the likelihood metric becomes a per-token weight rather than a dense coupling, so real and imaginary components are two features of each of 328 physical-frequency tokens, not 656 unrelated rows; this selects a physical token axis rather than proposing a learned discrete vocabulary. The model is a transformer \citep{vaswani2017attention} over frame patches; it is a vehicle, not a contribution (Appendix~\ref{app:rung4-baselines}).
