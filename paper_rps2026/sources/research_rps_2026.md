# R/PS 2026 venue research

Research date: 2026-08-07 (Asia/Shanghai)

Official page: https://representations-physical-sciences.github.io/workshop-2026/

The 2026 Representations for the Physical Sciences workshop is an in-person NeurIPS workshop in Paris on 12 or 13 December 2026. Its first edition is narrowly framed around representation learning for physical systems, particularly self-supervision, transfer, sampling, and tokenization. The call invites interdisciplinary work from core machine learning and AI for science.

Short papers are non-archival, double-blind, and limited to four pages of main content; references and optional appendices are excluded from the limit. The submission is one PDF through OpenReview, with no additional files. Properly anonymized repository links are allowed. The workshop does not require the NeurIPS main-track checklist. The target deadline is 29 August 2026 AoE and notification is due by 29 September 2026 AoE.

The official LaTeX template was downloaded from https://representations-physical-sciences.github.io/workshop-2026/assets/rps_2026_template.zip on 2026-08-07. It uses the NeurIPS 2026 `dblblindworkshop` option and sets the workshop footer through `\workshoptitle`.
