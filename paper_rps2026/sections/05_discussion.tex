\paragraph{Limitations.}
Rung 4 remains a two-seed pilot (Section~4). The linear-rung TIDMAD reversal is not replicated at a confirmatory file; stationary-PSD adequacy is per-instrument, holding on the TIDMAD readout but failing on GW strain; and one estimated-covariance condition narrows but does not remove the reliance on an oracle $\Sigma$ --- no sweep over calibration sizes or misspecification.
