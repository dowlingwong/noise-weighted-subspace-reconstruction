# G3 circle/fixation premise checked before D1

Source: Wakeman & Henson (2015), Methods, Experimental design and M/EEG acquisition: https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata20151 (doi:10.1038/sdata.2015.1), checked 2026-10-13.

The trial begins with a 400-600 ms fixation cross; the stimulus lasts 800-1000 ms; the interstimulus circle lasts 1700 ms. Participants may blink during the circle. Thus the registered development gap, from corrected onset +1.2 s to next corrected onset -0.05 s, may include circle/fixation transitions. This is a timing-based inference, not an empirical noise-gate conclusion. G3 remains the reviewed effect-size gate; no timing, threshold or kit edits.

The paper states a rounded 34 ms projector delay. The reviewed kit uses 34.5 ms with STI101/events cross-checks; the kit value is preserved.
