\paragraph{NFPA under channel compression.}
Rung 3 is consolidated at $C=8>k_c=2$ channels, matched rank $k=k_ck_t=6$: NFPA, an isotropically trained factored baseline of the same architecture, CW-PCA, and PCA on the same seeds. NFPA and the isotropic baseline differ only in the training metric, isolating it, and it reverses: in the frozen ten-seed experiment NFPA is better under the likelihood residual ($-0.0192$, 95\% interval $[-0.0194,-0.0190]$) and worse under raw MSE ($+0.0034$, $[+0.0034,+0.0034]$), both excluding zero, and neither marginal against seed-to-seed variation (paired $|d_z|>50$). An independent twenty-seed replication of the identical configuration agrees in sign and magnitude ($-0.0190$, $[-0.0192,-0.0189]$; $+0.0034$); cohorts are reported separately; a pooled figure would be post-hoc. Against unrestricted CW-PCA at matched rank NFPA's held-out residual is lower by $0.0026$, a finite-sample generalization difference rather than a superior restricted optimum (Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}). The twenty-seed white-noise run is an identity check: at $\Sigma=\sigma^2I$ the objectives coincide up to scale, and its exact zero shows no spurious contrast. Estimating the covariance from $2{,}048$ noise-only events preserves the reversal in all ten seeds ($-0.0191$, $[-0.0193,-0.0189]$; $+0.0034$), degrading NFPA's likelihood residual and raw MSE by $0.0078\%$ and $0.0345\%$ of its own oracle held-out values --- one fixed condition, not a robustness sweep (Table~\ref{tab:round10}).

\paragraph{Rung 4 (two-seed pilot): the metric in a factored-attention network.}
One factored-attention architecture is trained on the TIDMAD readout under two objectives differing only in the metric --- \texttt{T-mse} on the unwhitened spectrogram, \texttt{T-chi2} on the $\Sigma^{-1}$-weighted residual --- at matched data, budget and evaluation grid, at two independent seeds. \emph{The reversal does not appear at either seed}: the $\Sigma^{-1}$-trained arm is better on \emph{both} criteria in both runs ($-1.15$ and $-1.17$, both $\times10^{-4}$, on the likelihood residual; $-57.2$ and $-57.4$ on raw MSE, the latter opposite to prediction). A degenerate metric would explain that, and we tested for one: $J$ spans a factor $441$ and the whitening weights have coefficient of variation $0.69$, the same order as the rung-3 geometry where the reversal \emph{is} observed ($1.10$; white control $0$). Degeneracy is therefore not the explanation --- but the coarse score does not separate the arms ($-1.0\times10^{-3}$, intervals spanning zero) and both sit far below published baselines (Appendix~\ref{app:rung4-baselines}), so two seeds in a regime this score cannot resolve is an open observation about one instantiation, not evidence that architecture decides whether the metric matters.

\paragraph{What the covariance-weighted factors recover.}
The metric also changes what the representation \emph{is}, and these quantities are truth-referenced: unlike the ranking above, none of them follows from each model leading under its own objective. At the separable condition NFPA recovers the planted channel subspace almost exactly ($0.035$ projector error against the isotropic $1.021$) while temporal recovery stays weak ($0.768$ against $1.280$) --- an asymmetry a joint number would hide. All seven paired contrasts favour NFPA, every across-seed bootstrap interval excludes zero and all ten seeds agree in sign, with no multiplicity correction; two are predeclared (the channel and temporal projector errors) and five post-hoc. No recovery threshold was predeclared either way, so these are continuous estimates, not a verdict (Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}).

\paragraph{Transfer of the noise model.}
On a public gravitational-wave strain release \citep{gwoscgw150914,macleod2021gwpy}, a whitened score matching its reference form to $10^{-12}$ and calibrated on synthetic stationary noise (dispersion $1.15$) is about eight times as dispersed on real H1 and L1 strain ($9.42$, $8.83$; global-PSD arm $1.77$, $7.29$). A covariance model can be correctly implemented, validated synthetically, and still fail to transfer: implementation identity is not calibration.
