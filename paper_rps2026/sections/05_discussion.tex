\paragraph{Limitations.}
The ladder answers the metric question exactly at the linear rungs and empirically at rung 4, where the reversal is not observed. Rung 4 rests on two seeds per arm, bounding run-to-run variation without characterising it; both arms sit far below published full-band baselines, which are not comparable to these band-limited models; and the objectives there are measurably non-degenerate, so the absence is unexplained --- capacity, optimization trajectory and implicit regularization all remain candidates. Semantic recovery is strong on the channel axis, weak on the temporal one, its decoding half post-hoc with no predeclared threshold. The linear-rung TIDMAD reversal is not replicated at a confirmatory file; stationary-PSD adequacy is per-instrument (adequate on the TIDMAD readout, $\chi^2/\mathrm{dof}=1.0031$, failing on GW strain); and the $C=8>k_c=2$ result uses an oracle covariance, bounding what a correctly specified $\Sigma$ buys rather than an estimated one.
