## 1. Why this round exists

The September 2026 successor programme (T19-S…T22-S, CRESST-S) produced five audited outcomes: T19-S passed its observable-order and in-domain bound checks but the full sample covariance never reached the theorem domain; T20-S bracketed crossovers at theta=0.25/0.40 but the frozen scaling model failed at theta=0.12 (5/6, max standardized deviation 3.370 vs 2.984); T21-S failed H1 overall (296/298, both failures orthogonal/high-gamma at N=128) so the loadings step is untested; T22-S coverage failed in 13/65 non-drift scenarios and matched-comparison feasibility failed at 4/8/16 files, with the H5 random-effects summary omitted from the reduction; CRESST-S reproduced archived intervals bit-identically but two-run coverage is 0.44–0.61 under run heterogeneity.

This round does **not** try to make those failures disappear. Every protocol declares it was designed after the September results were observed; fresh confirmation can validate a new hypothesis but cannot retroactively preregister the old one. Historical verdicts (the T19 registered prediction NOT MET, the pulse-proxy c/N refutation, the 296/298 outcome and five unmet T21 predictions, all withheld T22 verdicts) are preserved verbatim and are explicitly not repaired.

### R1 — T22 small-file interval calibration (`r1_t22_calibration`) — ready to transfer, needs scientific review
- **Question:** can a prespecified interval achieve [0.925, 0.975] Wilson-band coverage at n_files=4/8/16 across the 65 non-drift scenarios while keeping type-I ≤ 0.07 with a coherent decision rule?
- **Design:** new protocol (T22-S v3) retaining the archived scenario grid, generators, effect scales, coverage band, Wilson-not-entirely-outside rule, type-I threshold and three-claim Holm family. Frozen candidate family of five: `two_stage` (archived baseline), `t_equal_file`, `two_stage_t`, `welch_fe_t`, `re_t` — each with declared target estimand and assumptions (`candidates.py` TARGET/ASSUMPTIONS). At most one primary replacement, selected on **fresh pilot seeds only** (8 pilot scenarios, 400 MC each) under a predeclared all-scenario adequacy rule (coverage band + type-I bound on every non-drift/null pilot scenario; tie-break: shortest median length); `"none"` is a valid freeze and is not repaired on confirmation. Confirmation: 69 scenarios × 10 chunks × 100 MC = 69,000 replicates (≥ the old 1,000/scenario precision). Candidates are scored paired on the same simulated replicate; unit of replication is a newly simulated file collection, not a bootstrap draw. Controls: candidate t interval reproduces the archived `t_interval`; coherent p equals the t pivot; interval ordering; targets documented. Drift scenarios are separately labeled stress tests and excluded from the selection rule.
- **Seeds:** development 920000–920999, pilot 21000–21299, confirmation 22000–22499 (disjointness tested cross-package and against archives).
- **Cost/execution:** ~73 h serial (68–80), anchor 72 h; decided **serial** and flagged borderline (A1). ~0.9 GB cell storage.
- **Review focus:** (i) the adequacy rule scores every candidate's coverage against the equal-file `true_mu`; for `re_t`/`welch_fe_t` this conflates target mismatch with miscalibration (R0 showed 29/65 scenarios have materially different targets). This is conservative but should be an explicit scientific-review decision: score such candidates against their own declared target, keep the equal-file scoring with the mismatch label, or drop them from primary selection. (ii) Pilot representativeness: 8 scenarios must carry an all-scenario selection rule for 65. (iii) Coherence requirement: if a candidate enters the claim rule, its p-value and Holm decision must match its interval — verify in `runner.py`.

### R2 — T20 scaling and crossover at theta=0.12 (`r2_t20_theta012`) — ready to transfer
- **Question:** (a) does the old frozen `c/N + d/N²` prediction replicate on fresh seeds at the old grid; (b) can a new model, selected only on fresh pilot seeds, predict `x_U(N)` and `d(N) − a_ref` over an extended grid; where is the crossover bracket?
- **Design:** imports the archived analytic fixed-spectrum generator unchanged (spectrum, trace, rank, SNR, gap invariant-checked per theta). Required geometries theta ∈ {0 (product control), 0.12 (primary), 0.25 (reference)}; theta=0.4 is a declared, costed **optional** expansion (`R2_T20_INCLUDE_OPTIONAL=1`), not required. N grid frozen before production: old [400…3200] plus extended [6400, 12800]. Two separated endpoints: (a) old frozen (c,d) rebuilt from original parameters with the CURRENT algebraic `a_ref` added **exactly once** (H7; double application rejected at reduce, exit 4; a dedicated `offset_applied_once` control verifies stored = base + a_ref and that base + 2·a_ref is numerically distinct); (b) new model selection on 16 fresh pilot seeds, split-half fit/holdout with both folds, candidates limited to the archived four {none, c/N, c/N^alpha, c/N+d/N²}, ≤2 parameters, no free intercept, alpha ∈ [0,4], and a complexity guard `min_relative_improvement = 0.01` fixed before any R2 data (explicitly **not** derived from the archived 2.7% margin). A `old_selection_margins` control re-verifies the 2.7%-vs-none / 0.43%-vs-c/N^alpha distinction. Inference: max-|t| seed bootstrap (2000 reps) jointly across the N grid, Bonferroni-t fallback (<8 seeds), 6-claim predeclared multiplicity family, all-of support rule; a crossing is bracketed only by adjacent decided opposite signs, never interpolated; no grid extension to chase a sign. 966 required cells (960 fit + 6 algebraic), 4,800 fits under a 7,000 cap.

### R3 — T21 finite-size recovery (`r3_t21_finite`) — ready to transfer
- **Question:** holding gamma=d/N, spike strengths and loading geometry fixed, does the archived isolated-spike×loadings predictor approach observed recovery as d,N scale together (d0=256 → 512 → 1024), and can the loadings step then be tested?
- **Design:** targeted matrix (6 grid configs + all-zero + all-strong per geometry × gamma arm × rung = 96 configurations/phase), **not** the old 104-config grid; conclusions explicitly targeted. d0=256 with hi-arm N=128 reproduces exactly the ambient size of both old primary failures; both old failure configuration types are included (`old_failure_subthreshold`, `old_failure_above`). Both gamma arms (2.0, 0.25), orthogonal and correlated Grams (frozen frame seed; Cholesky construction makes UᵀU exactly G at every d). Legacy baseline unchanged: dgj2018-eq1.6 predictor, 0.05 primary / 0.10 near-edge tolerances, [0.7,1.8] band — never widened to fix a failure. Predeclared finite-size endpoint: (decline) seed-mean |error| at 4d0 ≤ 0.05 AND strictly smaller than at d0 for both old-failure types; (agreement) every legacy-primary pair passes at 4d0. Gate: the orthogonal isolated-spike prerequisite must pass before a correlated-arm pass can support the loadings step; otherwise that step is marked **untested**. A separate labeled alternative-band candidate (6 frozen bands) may be selected on 12 pilot seeds and frozen before 24 confirmation seeds; the legacy classification is always reported beside it. Inference on seed means only (sd/se/fixed-seed bootstrap CI over seeds); no direction or bootstrap replicate treated as an independent sample. All-zero control checked against the exact Beta(k/2,(d−k)/2) chance law at z=5 (reduce refuses, exit 4, on violation). Executable ladder-preservation receipt at every rung (A3): realised Gram, unit norms, Schur identity, exact-K spectrum identical across rungs — `LADDER_PRESERVATION_RECEIPT.json`, 32 configs/rung, all pass.
- **Seeds:** dev 922000–922999, pilot 25000–25299, confirmation 26000–26499.
- **Cost/execution:** ~0.35 h serial (measured `BENCHMARK.json`: 3,456 fits, 816.9 s compute), anchor ≤7 h; serial.
- **Review focus:** that the old 296/298 outcome and five unmet predictions are restated not repaired; that the prerequisite gate cannot be bypassed; that the targeted scope is never presented as validation of the full old grid.

### R4 — T22 feasibility without unjustified file-count escalation (`r4_t22_feasibility`) — ready, cancellation-gated by R1
- **Question:** which evaluation-file counts and development-data conditions jointly meet the original feasibility rule (coverage in tolerance, type-I control, worst-case non-drift withholding ≤ 0.5, medium-effect **unconditional** power ≥ 0.5, ≥4 independent files)? Can the development gate make the criterion unreachable regardless of evaluation files?
- **Design:** two separable modules. (a) Evaluation grid: n_files ∈ {4,8,16,32,64} at a fixed development design (ndev4/ncal64), 16 scenarios. (b) Development-adequacy grid: ndev ∈ {4,8,16} × ncal ∈ {64,256} at fixed evaluation n=16, 12 scenarios — one-factor-comparable cells separating development-file count from calibration windows. Explicit **separate development/evaluation random streams** (`streams.py`; source change recorded; control: changing n_files leaves the development realization identical, and vice versa) — this removes the shared-RNG artifact that made old n_files comparisons realization-coupled. The exact bound P(supported) ≤ 1 − P(withheld) with Wilson uncertainty screens infeasible designs **before** pricing large-file production (H3). **A2 cancellation gate** (`r1_gate.py`): n_files=32/64 cells are admissible only if R1 froze an accepted candidate AND its acceptance receipt shows coverage/type-I pass at every file count AND the withholding screen is not universal; on cancel an `R4_INFEASIBLE` receipt is emitted and the cells are removed from matrix, launch plan and cost report — cancelled, not deferred, never staged ready-to-transfer; a mutation test proves the refusal fires. Fixed design: no selection authority, no pilot freeze. 50 MC/cell, 28,000 replicates total; conditional-on-not-withheld power is never a substitute.
- **Seeds:** dev 923000–923999, pilot 27000–27299, confirmation 28000–28499.
- **Cost/execution:** ~53 h serial full (anchor 67 h, which covered only the eval module); ~28 h if screening/gate removes n=32/64; serial.
- **Review focus:** the default freeze/acceptance paths in the protocol point into the source tree and are only correct on the remote via the documented `R4_R1_FREEZE`/`R4_R1_ACCEPTANCE` env overrides (the gate fails closed to "unresolved" if absent — safe, but worth a path-convention note); the stream-separation control; that "stabilised" is never forced in scientific runs.
