\paragraph{Limitations.}
Rung 4 is a two-seed pilot in an unresolved regime (Section~4). The linear-rung TIDMAD reversal is not replicated at a confirmatory file; stationary-PSD adequacy is per-instrument, holding on the TIDMAD readout and failing on GW strain; and one estimated-covariance condition narrows, but does not remove, the primary experiment's reliance on an oracle $\Sigma$ --- no sweep over calibration sizes or misspecification.
