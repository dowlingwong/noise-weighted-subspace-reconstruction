The attention model is a transformer \citep{vaswani2017attention} over frame patches \citep{nie2023patchtst}, with rotary temporal embeddings \citep{su2024roformer} and learned band embeddings, trained under two objectives differing only in the metric --- \texttt{T-mse} on the unwhitened spectrogram, \texttt{T-chi2} on the $\Sigma^{-1}$-weighted residual --- at matched data, budget and evaluation grid.

\subsection{Predeclared confirmatory protocol}
\label{app:protocol}
Mean handling, all experiments: the training mean is estimated once, applied unchanged to held-out observations, and added back after reconstruction.

The multi-seed confirmatory study predeclared three hypotheses. \textbf{Executed:} H1 (metric reversal at fixed architecture): under colored or correlated noise, NFPA and an isotropically trained multilinear projection with the same $(k_c,k_t)$ should disagree in ranking, with paired across-seed confidence intervals excluding zero in the predicted directions. H2 (white-noise identity check): when $\Sigma=\sigma^2I$ the two objectives coincide up to scale, so covariance-aware and Euclidean projectors must agree to numerical tolerance; this validates the pipeline rather than the hypothesis. \textbf{Executed after the main blocks:} H3 (active restriction): with $C>k_c$ and $T>k_t$, NFPA should recover the planted channel and temporal subspaces for separable signals. This was not run with the original blocks; it was executed afterwards on the archived fits, each of which reproduces its archived held-out residual to $\leq10^{-9}$ relative, so the analysis is computed on the archived fits rather than on refits that merely resemble them. Because the protocol set no numerical recovery threshold, the result is reported as continuous projector errors with bootstrap intervals that resample seeds and no binary verdict. Truth-referenced parameter decoding (amplitude, rise time, decay time) was proposed in a block dropped before execution; it is reported at the archived $\alpha=0.1$ condition as \emph{post-hoc exploratory}, and at the separable $\alpha=0$ condition alongside the H3 analysis. An earlier design specified 30 seeds; the executed, frozen protocol is 10 seeds, amended before any confirmatory result existed. The primary configuration uses $C=8$ channels, $T=256$ samples, ranks $(k_c,k_t)=(2,3)$, $800/400/2000$ training/validation/test events, $10$ independent seeds, $10$ ALS restarts per seed, SNR $10$ under $\mathrm{SNR}^2=\mathbb{E}[s^\top\Sigma^{-1}s]$, Brownian $1/f^2$ temporal noise with channel correlation, oracle covariance, and nonseparability $\alpha=0.1$. Results enter the main text only if every acceptance gate holds: all seeds complete without post-result configuration changes; the fixed-architecture reversal intervals exclude zero in the predicted directions; the white-noise check passes its predeclared equivalence margin; and the table and figure are generated directly from archived seed-level results.

\paragraph{Standardized effect size at rung 3.} The relative differences in Section~4 are small against the \emph{level} of the normalized residual ($\bar\chi^2\approx1.014$) and large against seed-to-seed variation. With $d_z$ the mean paired difference divided by the standard deviation of the per-seed differences, the frozen cohort gives $d_z=-55.1$ on $\bar\chi^2$ and $+99.5$ on raw MSE, the replication cohort $-66.2$ and $+71.2$, and the estimated-covariance condition $-52.6$ and $+93.6$. Conventionally $|d_z|=0.8$ is a large effect. This is also why every cohort has $\text{frac}(\text{sign agrees})=1$: the reversal is essentially deterministic across seeds at this configuration. It is a statement about consistency, not about practical importance at any particular instrument.

\paragraph{Rung 3 against the unrestricted rung-2 optimum.} At the frozen $C=8>k_c=2$ configuration NFPA's held-out likelihood residual is lower than unrestricted CW-PCA's by $0.0026$ ($[0.0025,0.0026]$). This held-out finite-sample difference does not imply that a restricted training optimum beats the unrestricted one: Proposition~\ref{prop:restricted} compares the fitted objective over nested classes and gives the condition for equal optima.

\paragraph{Semantic recovery, per method.} At the separable condition NFPA attains amplitude RMSE $0.143$ against the isotropic baseline's $0.198$ (decoded-amplitude correlation $0.70$ against $0.13$), and lower rise- and decay-time errors. The same analysis at the archived $\alpha=0.1$ condition is post-hoc exploratory and agrees in direction on all seven contrasts.

The factored representation (Section~3, generic form) has $O(k_1n_1+k_2n_2)$ basis parameters rather than $O(k_1k_2n_1n_2)$ for an unstructured basis, trading approximation flexibility for a two-axis inductive bias related to classical tensor factorizations \citep{kolda2009,lu2008mpca}. Alternating spectral updates solve each factor subproblem exactly, although the joint product-manifold objective remains nonconvex.

\subsection{Transfer of the noise model}
\label{app:transfer}

On a public gravitational-wave strain release \citep{gwoscgw150914,macleod2021gwpy}, a whitened score matching its reference form to $10^{-12}$ and calibrated on synthetic stationary noise (dispersion $1.15$) is seven to nine times as dispersed on real H1 and L1 strain ($9.42$ and $8.83$; the global-PSD arm gives $1.77$ and $7.29$). The claim is about covariance adequacy on that instrument, not about NFPA and not about detector sensitivity.

\subsection{Estimated covariance, and a corrected angle diagnostic}
\label{app:round10}

\input{tables/round10_sensitivity}

The estimated-covariance condition repeats the frozen $C=8>k_c=2$ configuration with one change: the separable covariance is estimated from $N_{\mathrm{cal}}=2048$ independent noise-only calibration events instead of being supplied as an oracle. Estimation proceeds by sample estimation of each factor, the regularization already used for the oracle path, and a symmetric product-scale calibration $\hat v=\sqrt{v_cv_t}$ with each factor divided by $\sqrt{\hat v}$; that correction was frozen before any full result existed. The reversal survives in all ten seeds, and the degradation relative to NFPA's own oracle held-out values is small but resolved --- consistent with the fit being invariant to a global whitening scale (the estimated product scale is within $0.1\%$ of oracle) while the covariance \emph{shape} is estimated accurately. This is a single condition: it does not bound behaviour across calibration sizes, covariance families or deliberate misspecification, and no such sweep is claimed.

A diagnostic correction was also applied. The helper computing principal angles unstacked a whitened basis in the wrong order, mixing channel and temporal indices; it affected only the isotropic baseline's angle diagnostic and neither primary metric. Recomputing leaves NFPA's mean angle unchanged and moves the isotropic baseline by under one degree in both cohorts, so no conclusion depended on it. The corrected values are the ones reported; the original implementation is retained unchanged so that previously published tables keep their meaning. The correction is post-hoc, contributes no confirmatory evidence, and is not a second attention-class seed.

\paragraph{Rung-2 numbers carried from an earlier study.}
In the 30-seed rank-two benchmark ($512$ traces of dimension $256$ per seed), PCA and CW-PCA had held-out
weighted residuals $48.419$ and $47.869$ (paired difference $0.550$, $[0.530,0.571]$) while raw MSE
preferred the opposite reconstruction, their whitened subspaces $86.25^\circ$ apart. The ten-seed
white-noise control gave a maximum angle of $1.48\times10^{-6}$ degrees. On the TIDMAD file the amplitude
RMSE fell from $0.1209$ to $0.0880$.

\subsection{Sanity check: the earlier single-split study}
\label{app:single-split}

This predates the primary experiment and does \emph{not} compress the channel axis; it is retained
as an implementation sanity check, not as evidence. It uses $C=2$ channels, $T=256$ samples,
$1\,\mathrm{kHz}$ sampling, channel weights $(1.0,0.65)$, amplitude $A\sim\mathcal{N}(1,0.3^2)$,
Brownian $S_n(f)\propto1/f^2$ noise, and $800/300$ train/test events; NFPA and IsoAE use
$(k_c,k_t)=(2,3)$, CW-PCA and IsoPCA rank six.

Because $C=k_c=2$, $U_cU_c^\top=I_2$ here: the reconstruction restricts a shared temporal subspace
but does not compress channels. Principal angles between NFPA and CW-PCA are $0.0^\circ$,
$59.3^\circ$, $64.6^\circ$, $73.8^\circ$, $84.8^\circ$ and $87.8^\circ$ (mean $61.7^\circ$) --- the
counterexample showing that a full-rank factor does not deactivate the restriction. The IsoAE--CW-PCA
angles are $82.1^\circ$, $83.7^\circ$, $86.0^\circ$, $86.6^\circ$, $88.2^\circ$ and $89.4^\circ$
(mean $86.0^\circ$). These are point estimates from one seed and split, computed by a different code
path from the multi-seed cohorts, so the corrected angles of Table~\ref{tab:round10} are not
replacements for them. Held-out raw MSE for IsoAE and IsoPCA is $4.58\times10^{-9}$ and
$4.33\times10^{-9}$.
