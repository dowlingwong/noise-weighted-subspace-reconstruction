\paragraph{Limitations.}
The ladder answers the metric question in closed form at the linear rungs and empirically at rung 4, where the reversal is not observed. Four caveats bound that evidence. Rung 4 is one seed per arm, so its held-out differences are contrasts over evaluations rather than evidence about seed variability; both arms sit far below the published baselines; and the objectives there are measurably non-degenerate, so what does account for the absence is unknown. The linear-rung TIDMAD reversal is not replicated at a confirmatory file. Stationary-PSD adequacy is per-instrument: adequate on the TIDMAD readout ($\chi^2/\mathrm{dof}=1.0031$), failing on gravitational-wave strain. The $C=8>k_c=2$ result uses an oracle covariance, bounding what a correctly specified $\Sigma$ buys rather than an estimated one.
