\section{Covariance-weighted representation geometry}

Let $x_i=s_i+n_i\in\R^d$ be mean-subtracted with $n_i\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\Sigma)$, $\Sigma\succ0$ (mean handling in Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}). For a candidate $\hat{s}_i$ the Gaussian negative log-likelihood is, up to constants,
\begin{equation}
  \chi^2(\hat{s}_i)=(x_i-\hat{s}_i)^\top\Sigma^{-1}(x_i-\hat{s}_i).
  \label{eq:mahalanobis}
\end{equation}
Whitening, $\tilde{x}_i=\Sigma^{-1/2}x_i$, maps this form to $\|\tilde{x}_i-\hat{\tilde{s}}_i\|_2^2$: Euclidean projection in whitened coordinates, $\Sigma^{-1}$-orthogonal projection in measurement coordinates. We report the per-coordinate normalized residual $\bar\chi^2=(Nd)^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\chi^2(\hat{s}_i)$, or the per-trace mean where stated.

Why label-free learning works: at the population level, for independent zero-mean signal and noise, $\mathrm{Cov}(\Sigma^{-1/2}x)=\mathrm{Cov}(\Sigma^{-1/2}s)+I$ (Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}); adding $I$ shifts eigenvalues, not eigenvectors, so the leading whitened eigenspace of the \emph{noisy} data is the signal's own; at finite $N$ the sample eigenvectors are perturbed. In practice $\Sigma$ comes from calibration or noise-only records, estimated without evaluation data; a misspecified $\Sigma$ is a misspecified geometry.

\begin{proposition}[Noise-weighted projection bridge]
\label{prop:bridge}
Under the model above, OF, rank-$k$ covariance-weighted PCA (CW-PCA), and a tied noise-aware linear autoencoder minimize Eq.~\eqref{eq:mahalanobis} over different admissible subspaces. OF fixes a rank-one template; CW-PCA learns a rank-$k$ subspace; and the tied autoencoder parameterizes the same learned projection when its encoder is the $\Sigma^{-1}$-adjoint of its decoder.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Proof sketch.}
Whitening reduces the problem to ordinary rank-$k$ approximation, so every minimizer spans a leading eigenspace of the whitened sample covariance; the tied $\Sigma^{-1}$-adjoint encoder follows. Projection onto a \emph{fixed} rank-one template yields the OF amplitude; rank-one CW-PCA instead \emph{learns} the leading whitened direction, and the two coincide under the separable-template and eigengap assumptions of Corollary~\ref{cor:rankone} (Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}).

\paragraph{Metric reversal.}
Raw MSE replaces $\Sigma^{-1}$ by $I$ and is likelihood-correct only when $\Sigma=\sigma^2I$; under colored noise MSE follows raw variance while Eq.~\eqref{eq:mahalanobis} emphasizes high-information directions. Consequently, two equal-rank models can satisfy $\mathrm{MSE}(A)<\mathrm{MSE}(B)$ but $\chi^2(A)>\chi^2(B)$: each is optimal in a different geometry.
