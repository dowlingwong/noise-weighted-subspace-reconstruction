\section{Evidence}

\paragraph{Metric reversal in the linear rungs.}
A 30-seed rank-two synthetic benchmark and a ten-seed white-noise control establish the reversal at rung 2, and on one public TIDMAD file with an injected reference \citep{fry2024tidmad} the weighted residual and the recorded truth agree against raw MSE --- one file at one operating point, not replicated at a confirmatory file. Numbers in Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}. The consolidated evidence below supersedes these as the paper's linear-rung result.

\paragraph{Why the architecture binds next.}
TIDMAD's diagnostics show the real signal is not a fixed low-dimensional subspace --- the tone sweeps, one file is two-tone, and the analytic rank-two prediction failed on three files for diagnosed non-implementation reasons --- so the architecture, not the metric, is what binds; rung 4 lifts it.

\paragraph{NFPA under channel compression.}
Rung 3 is consolidated on a non-degenerate configuration with $C=8>k_c=2$ channels and matched total rank $k=k_ck_t=6$: NFPA, an isotropically trained factored baseline of the same architecture, CW-PCA, and ordinary PCA are fitted at matched rank on the same ten seeds. Because NFPA and the isotropic baseline share an architecture and differ only in the training metric, their paired contrast isolates the metric. It reverses: NFPA is better under the likelihood residual (paired difference $-0.0192$, seed-level bootstrap 95\% interval $[-0.0194,-0.0190]$) and worse under raw MSE ($+0.0034$, $[+0.0034,+0.0034]$), both intervals excluding zero over ten seeds. Against the unrestricted CW-PCA the held-out likelihood residual differs by $-0.0026$ ($[-0.0026,-0.0025]$); this is a finite-sample held-out difference, not evidence that the Kronecker restriction is intrinsically superior. In the twenty-seed white-noise control both contrasts are zero to machine precision ($0.000000$, interval $[0,0]$), isolating covariance geometry rather than rank or optimization as the cause of the separation. The single-split $C=k_c=2$ run is retained in Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}.

\paragraph{Rung 4: the metric in a factored-attention network.}
One factored-attention architecture is trained on the TIDMAD readout with two objectives differing only in the metric --- \texttt{T-mse} on the unwhitened spectrogram, \texttt{T-chi2} on the $\Sigma^{-1}$-weighted residual --- at matched data, budget, seed handling and evaluation grid. \emph{The reversal does not appear at this rung.} Over fifty held-out evaluations the $\Sigma^{-1}$-trained arm is better on \emph{both} criteria: paired difference $-1.15\times10^{-4}$ on the normalized likelihood residual ($[-1.27,-1.03]\times10^{-4}$) and $-57.2$ on raw MSE ($[-71.6,-42.6]$), both intervals excluding zero but the second in the direction opposite to prediction. The release's coarse denoising score does not separate the arms: the paired difference is $-1.0\times10^{-3}$ with window and file-block bootstrap intervals of $[-3.3,+1.4]$ and $[-4.6,+2.4]\times10^{-3}$, both spanning zero, and both arms sit far below the published baselines (Appendix~\ref{app:rung4-baselines}); Figure~\ref{fig:evidence} shows all three contrasts together. A degenerate metric would explain it, and we tested for one: $J$ spans a factor $441$ over the modelled band and the whitening weights have coefficient of variation $0.69$, far from proportional. We read the absence as a property of this instantiation, not of the ladder: at this capacity and budget the architecture absorbs the weighting, with one seed per arm.

\paragraph{Covariance adequacy on real data.}
We check calibration on a public gravitational-wave strain release \citep{gwoscgw150914,macleod2021gwpy}. The whitened score matches its reference form to $10^{-12}$ or better and is calibrated on stationary synthetic noise (dispersion $1.15$), yet on real H1 and L1 strain it is seven to nine times over-dispersed ($9.42$, $8.83$), which a global leave-one-out variant does not rescue ($1.77$, $7.29$). A covariance model can be correctly implemented, validated synthetically, and still fail calibration on real data: implementation identity is not calibration.
