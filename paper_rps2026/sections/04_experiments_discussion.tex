\section{Evidence}

\paragraph{Metric reversal in the linear rungs.}
In a 30-seed rank-two benchmark, PCA and CW-PCA had held-out weighted residuals $48.419$ and $47.869$ (paired difference $0.550$, $[0.530,0.571]$) while raw MSE preferred the opposite reconstruction, their whitened subspaces $86.25^\circ$ apart; the ten-seed white-noise control gave $1.48\times10^{-6}$ degrees. On one public TIDMAD file with an injected reference \citep{fry2024tidmad} the same ranking held and the amplitude RMSE fell from $0.1209$ to $0.0880$ --- one file at one operating point, not replicated at a confirmatory file.

\paragraph{Why the architecture binds next.}
TIDMAD's diagnostics show the real signal is not a fixed low-dimensional subspace --- the tone sweeps, one file is two-tone, and the analytic rank-two prediction failed on three files for diagnosed non-implementation reasons --- so the architecture, not the metric, is what binds; rung 4 lifts it.

\paragraph{NFPA under channel compression.}
Rung 3 is consolidated on a non-degenerate configuration with $C=8>k_c=2$ channels and matched total rank $k=k_ck_t=6$: NFPA, an isotropically trained factored baseline of the same architecture, CW-PCA, and ordinary PCA are fitted at matched rank on the same ten seeds. Because NFPA and the isotropic baseline share an architecture and differ only in the training metric, their paired contrast isolates the metric. It reverses: NFPA is better under the likelihood residual (paired difference $-0.0192$, seed-level bootstrap 95\% interval $[-0.0194,-0.0190]$) and worse under raw MSE ($+0.0034$, $[+0.0034,+0.0034]$), both intervals excluding zero over ten seeds. Against the unrestricted CW-PCA the held-out likelihood residual differs by $-0.0026$ ($[-0.0026,-0.0025]$); this is a finite-sample held-out difference, not evidence that the Kronecker restriction is intrinsically superior. In the twenty-seed white-noise control both contrasts are zero to machine precision ($0.000000$, interval $[0,0]$), isolating covariance geometry rather than rank or optimization as the cause of the separation. The single-split $C=k_c=2$ run is retained in Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}.

\paragraph{Rung 4: the metric in a factored-attention network.}
One factored-attention architecture is trained on the TIDMAD readout with two objectives differing only in the metric --- \texttt{T-mse} on the unwhitened spectrogram, \texttt{T-chi2} on the $\Sigma^{-1}$-weighted residual --- at matched data, budget, seed handling and evaluation grid. \emph{The reversal does not appear at this rung.} Over fifty held-out evaluations the $\Sigma^{-1}$-trained arm is better on \emph{both} criteria: paired difference $-1.15\times10^{-4}$ on the normalized likelihood residual ($[-1.27,-1.03]\times10^{-4}$) and $-57.2$ on raw MSE ($[-71.6,-42.6]$), both intervals excluding zero but the second in the direction opposite to prediction. The release's coarse denoising score does not separate the arms at all: the paired difference is $-1.0\times10^{-3}$ with a window bootstrap interval $[-3.3,+1.4]\times10^{-3}$ and a file-block interval $[-4.6,+2.4]\times10^{-3}$, both spanning zero, and both arms sit far below the published baselines (Appendix~\ref{app:rung4-baselines}). The likely reason the objectives fail to separate is that whitening the band-limited spectrogram is close to a constant rescaling over the modelled band, leaving the two losses nearly collinear --- a property of this instantiation rather than of the ladder.

\paragraph{Covariance adequacy on real data.}
We check calibration against a public gravitational-wave strain release \citep{gwoscgw150914} with a shared-statistic filter \citep{macleod2021gwpy}. The whitened score agrees with its reference form to $10^{-12}$ or better and is calibrated on stationary synthetic noise (dispersion $1.15$), yet on real H1 and L1 strain the same model is seven to nine times over-dispersed ($9.42$, $8.83$), and a global leave-one-out variant does not rescue it ($1.77$, $7.29$). A covariance model can therefore be correctly implemented, validated on synthetic data, and still fail calibration on real data: implementation identity is not calibration.
