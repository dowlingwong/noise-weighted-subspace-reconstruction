\paragraph{Limitations.}
The ladder answers the metric question exactly at the linear rungs and exploratorily at rung 4, where the reversal is not observed. Four caveats bound that evidence. Rung 4 is one seed per arm --- its differences describe two runs, not the architecture; both arms sit far below published full-band baselines, not directly comparable to these band-limited models; and the objectives there are measurably non-degenerate, so the absence is unexplained (capacity, optimization trajectory, and implicit regularization remain candidates). The linear-rung TIDMAD reversal is not replicated at a confirmatory file. Stationary-PSD adequacy is per-instrument: adequate on the TIDMAD readout ($\chi^2/\mathrm{dof}=1.0031$), failing on gravitational-wave strain. The $C=8>k_c=2$ result uses an oracle covariance: it bounds what a correctly specified $\Sigma$ buys, not an estimated one.
