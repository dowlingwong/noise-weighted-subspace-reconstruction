\paragraph{Limitations.}
Rung 4 rests on two seeds per arm, bounding run-to-run variation without characterising it, and its band-limited arms are not comparable to the published full-band baselines; since the objectives there are measurably non-degenerate, the absence remains unexplained --- capacity, optimization trajectory and implicit regularization all remain candidates. Semantic recovery is strong on the channel axis, weak on the temporal one, its decoding half post-hoc with no predeclared threshold. The linear-rung TIDMAD reversal is not replicated at a confirmatory file; stationary-PSD adequacy is per-instrument (adequate on the TIDMAD readout, $\chi^2/\mathrm{dof}=1.0031$, failing on GW strain); and the primary experiment's oracle covariance is narrowed, not removed, by that single estimated-covariance condition --- no sweep over calibration sizes, covariance families or misspecification is claimed. \looseness=-1
