\paragraph{Limitations.}
Rung 4 is a two-seed pilot in an unresolved regime (Section~4). Semantic recovery is asymmetric; the linear-rung TIDMAD reversal is not replicated at a confirmatory file; stationary-PSD adequacy is per-instrument, adequate on the TIDMAD readout and failing on GW strain; and the primary experiment's oracle covariance is narrowed, not removed, by one estimated-covariance condition --- no sweep over calibration sizes, covariance families or misspecification.
