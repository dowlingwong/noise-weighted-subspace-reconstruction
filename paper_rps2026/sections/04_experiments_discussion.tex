\section{Evidence}

\paragraph{Metric reversal in the linear rungs.}
A 30-seed rank-two synthetic benchmark and a ten-seed white-noise control establish the reversal at rung 2; on one public TIDMAD file with an injected reference \citep{fry2024tidmad} the weighted residual agrees with the recorded truth against raw MSE --- one file, not replicated at a confirmatory file (Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}). The consolidated evidence below supersedes these. TIDMAD's diagnostics also show the signal is not a fixed low-dimensional subspace (the tone sweeps; one file is two-tone), so the architecture binds; rung 4 lifts it.

\paragraph{NFPA under channel compression.}
Rung 3 is consolidated at $C=8>k_c=2$ channels and matched total rank $k=k_ck_t=6$: NFPA, an isotropically trained factored baseline of the same architecture, CW-PCA, and ordinary PCA, fitted on the same ten seeds. Because NFPA and the isotropic baseline share an architecture and differ only in the training metric, their paired contrast isolates the metric. It reverses: NFPA is better under the likelihood residual (paired difference $-0.0192$, seed-level bootstrap 95\% interval $[-0.0194,-0.0190]$) and worse under raw MSE ($+0.0034$, $[+0.0034,+0.0034]$), both intervals excluding zero over ten seeds. Against the unrestricted CW-PCA the held-out likelihood residual differs by $-0.0026$ ($[-0.0026,-0.0025]$) --- a finite-sample difference, not evidence the Kronecker restriction is intrinsically superior. In the twenty-seed white-noise control both contrasts are zero to machine precision ($0.000000$, $[0,0]$), isolating covariance geometry rather than rank or optimization as the cause. The single-split $C=k_c=2$ run is in Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}.

\paragraph{Rung 4: the metric in a factored-attention network.}
One factored-attention architecture is trained on the TIDMAD readout with two objectives differing only in the metric --- \texttt{T-mse} on the unwhitened spectrogram, \texttt{T-chi2} on the $\Sigma^{-1}$-weighted residual --- at matched data, budget, seed handling and evaluation grid. \emph{The reversal does not appear at this rung.} Over fifty held-out evaluations the $\Sigma^{-1}$-trained arm is better on \emph{both} criteria: paired difference $-1.15\times10^{-4}$ on the normalized likelihood residual ($[-1.27,-1.03]\times10^{-4}$) and $-57.2$ on raw MSE ($[-71.6,-42.6]$), both intervals excluding zero but the second in the direction opposite to prediction. These evaluations lie along one training trajectory and are autocorrelated, so the intervals understate uncertainty; the last-ten contrast agrees in sign on both metrics. The release's coarse denoising score does not separate the arms: paired difference $-1.0\times10^{-3}$, window and file-block bootstrap intervals $[-3.3,+1.4]$ and $[-4.6,+2.4]\times10^{-3}$, both spanning zero; both arms sit far below the published baselines (Appendix~\ref{app:rung4-baselines}); Figure~\ref{fig:evidence} shows all three contrasts. A degenerate metric would explain it, and we tested for one: $J$ spans a factor $441$ over the modelled band and the whitening weights have coefficient of variation $0.69$ --- the same order as the synthetic rung-3 geometry where the reversal \emph{is} observed ($1.10$; the white control is $0$ by construction) --- so the metric is comparably non-degenerate at both rungs, and we read the absence as a property of this instantiation, not of the ladder: at this capacity and budget the architecture absorbs the weighting, with one seed per arm.

\paragraph{Transfer of the noise model.}
On a public gravitational-wave strain release \citep{gwoscgw150914,macleod2021gwpy}, a whitened score matching its reference form to $10^{-12}$ and calibrated on stationary synthetic noise (dispersion $1.15$) is seven to nine times over-dispersed on real H1 and L1 strain ($9.42$, $8.83$; leave-one-out $1.77$, $7.29$). A covariance model can be correctly implemented, validated synthetically, and still fail to transfer: implementation identity is not calibration, and the transfer step is where a learned reconstruction geometry is falsified.
